# Periods and comparisons

## Reporting periods
All periods are anchored on the latest data week. L4W is the last four weeks; P4W the four weeks before them;
L4W_YA the same four weeks a year earlier (52 weeks back). L12W and L12W_YA are the last twelve weeks and the same
weeks a year ago. L52W and L52W_YA are the last 52 weeks and the 52 weeks before that.

## Comparison codes
L4W_VS_YA compares the last four weeks with the same weeks a year ago. L4W_VS_PP compares them with the previous
four weeks. L12W_VS_YA compares the last twelve weeks with a year ago. L52W_VS_YA compares the latest year with
the year before. Every answer names its comparison, because a brand can gain on one and lose on another.

## History needed
Year-ago comparisons need at least 56 weeks of history for L4W_VS_YA, 64 for L12W_VS_YA and 104 for L52W_VS_YA.
With less, the comparison is not available and Share Radar says so rather than estimating.

## The replay control
For the demo, Share Radar can replay real NIQ weeks in order. The setting AS_OF_WEEK_END hides weeks after it;
"Load next week" moves it forward one real week, and the whole pipeline runs as if that week had just arrived.
